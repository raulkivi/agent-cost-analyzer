Board notes

U9: what to check

  • Basis and currency on every figure. The first tile is the preferred basis, with the turns it covers. Billed, reported and priced follow, each with its own coverage. A currency is a line of its own: AI Credits and USD are never added.
  • One currency at a time in the chart. The segmented control switches the chart and the vendor and session figures between the result's native currencies. USD is the result's own reported figure, not a conversion.
  • Price-change markers. A solid line and filled diamond is a documented change. A dashed line, hollow diamond and the "inferred" tag is an observed one, drawn from the week it began.
  • Partly known weeks are hatched, and the note says how many turns are known. A week with no turns reads "0 turns", never an empty gap.
  • Repositories show the folder name only. A repository result has no top sessions, so U1 is reached from the project view.
  • Drill-down: a top session opens U1 in place (see Increment 2 states).
  • Host (its recorded variables): page and card backgrounds, text, borders, fonts, radii, and the semantic colours. The raise and lower bars in the waterfall use the host's danger and info text colours, and always carry the written sign.
  • Brand (brand.css, follows hostContext.theme): the 16 px mark, the kicker, the four corner marks, and the data marks: columns, share bars, the total bars in the waterfall, and the price-change markers.
  • Headings use the host font, as in the A0b boards. No emoji, no host logos, no brand fills on large areas.
