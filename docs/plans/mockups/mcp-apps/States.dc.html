Summary

What the states prove

  • A null measure reads Unknown, never 0, in tiles, chart and tables (constraint 6).
  • Partial coverage writes "known for N of M turns" under the figure it qualifies.
  • "estimate" sits on every estimated figure.
  • A failed app-only call (get_turn_series) degrades one section; a failed tool replaces the body with its reason.
  • At 360 px the tiles wrap 2 × 2, the chart keeps every turn, and the top-turns table becomes a list.
  • AI Credits Analyzer (extension, v0.4.0) shows the copy button; Agent Cost Analyzer (standalone, v0.11.0) has no panel to open, so the button is hidden.
