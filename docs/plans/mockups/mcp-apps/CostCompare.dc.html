Board notes

U10: what to check

  • Columns: total, change, cost per session, per active day, per turn, and cache-hit ratio, as compare_costs returns them. The share bar sits under the total, as a share of the compared ids together.
  • Change shows the ratio and the absolute difference, signed. A row with nothing known for the previous period reads "no earlier figure", never "from 0".
  • Together is the result's total. The tool returns no combined change, so that cell stays empty rather than being computed in the view.
  • Coverage: a row with unknown turns carries "31/44" beside its total, with the tooltip "known for 31 of 44 turns".
  • The period in the header is the result's own dates, for this period and the previous one.
  • Click a row to open U9 for that id in place (see Increment 2 states).
  • Host (its recorded variables): page and card backgrounds, text, borders, fonts, radii, and the semantic colours. The raise and lower bars in the waterfall use the host's danger and info text colours, and always carry the written sign.
  • Brand (brand.css, follows hostContext.theme): the 16 px mark, the kicker, the four corner marks, and the data marks: columns, share bars, the total bars in the waterfall, and the price-change markers.
  • Headings use the host font, as in the A0b boards. No emoji, no host logos, no brand fills on large areas.
