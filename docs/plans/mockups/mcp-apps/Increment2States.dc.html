Board notes

Display rules, as drawn

  • No projects (6). The extension has no project file. list_projects returns the status and the reason text shown; get_project_cost answers an unknown-id error. The view puts the reason where the chart would be. Repositories remain available.
  • No earlier figure (7). A change against a period with nothing known is null in the tool result, so the cell reads "no earlier figure". There is no "from 0" and no percentage.
  • Unknown and partial (8). "Unknown" in italics for a figure with no value, "known for N of M turns" for partial ones, hatched bars for partly known weeks. Never 0.
  • Mixed currencies (9). AI Credits and USD stay on their own lines, USD with a "$". Together has one total per currency.
  • Error (10, 14). The message names the tool and the cause, and what to do. A failed drill-down keeps "Back to comparison".
  • Narrow (11 to 13). The card goes to one column. In U10 the per-session, per-day, per-turn and cache figures move under the row. Chart labels thin out to every third week.
  • Host (its recorded variables): page and card backgrounds, text, borders, fonts, radii, and the semantic colours. The raise and lower bars in the waterfall use the host's danger and info text colours, and always carry the written sign.
  • Brand (brand.css, follows hostContext.theme): the 16 px mark, the kicker, the four corner marks, and the data marks: columns, share bars, the total bars in the waterfall, and the price-change markers.
  • Headings use the host font, as in the A0b boards. No emoji, no host logos, no brand fills on large areas.
