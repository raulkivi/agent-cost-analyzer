Board notes

U11: what to check

  • The waterfall adds up. August, then usage, price and mix, then October. The three effects sum exactly to the change; the tool refuses a result that does not.
  • Priced basis only. explain_cost_change always uses tokens times the price version in force at the time, so the view says so and shows no basis switch.
  • Per-model table. Each row opens to token types with tokens and unit prices (AI Credits per 1M tokens, A to B) and the price versions behind them. A model's usage includes the work it gained or lost, so the row sums to its change and the mix effect sits only in the totals.
  • Not explained counts turns with no priced figure per period, with the tool's reasons. They are never counted as 0.
  • Price changes is a control only. It opens U13 in place, filtered to the models with the largest price effect. U13 is drawn in A25b; this board does not show what it opens.
  • Raise and lower use the host's danger and info text colours as well as the written sign, so colour is never the only cue.
  • Host (its recorded variables): page and card backgrounds, text, borders, fonts, radii, and the semantic colours. The raise and lower bars in the waterfall use the host's danger and info text colours, and always carry the written sign.
  • Brand (brand.css, follows hostContext.theme): the 16 px mark, the kicker, the four corner marks, and the data marks: columns, share bars, the total bars in the waterfall, and the price-change markers.
  • Headings use the host font, as in the A0b boards. No emoji, no host logos, no brand fills on large areas.
