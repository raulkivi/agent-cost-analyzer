Board notes

U12: what to check

  • Simulated everywhere. The title, the replay tile and the chart carry the "Simulated" tag; bars are hatched, measured is solid, and the prose says "would have cost about". Nothing simulated looks like a billed figure.
  • Self-check on top. A failed self-check is a red-bordered alert above the figures with the counts (37 of 44 requests). A passed one is a quiet line. A missing self-check would read "not available" with its reason.
  • Partial replay. Rows and tiles with turns not replayed show "≥" and "known for 40 of 42 turns"; the change column says "partial" rather than a percent. The two turns are listed with their reason and left as a gap in the chart.
  • Colour is never the only cue. Measured is a solid bar or a solid line with filled dots; simulated is a hatched bar or a dashed line with open diamonds. Both lines carry a direct label.
  • Drill-down. A click on a model row calls replay_session through the host and swaps to replay mode with "Back to model comparison".
Board notes

Money and the host frames

  • Every figure names AI Credits and its basis ("billed" for measured). Simulated figures name the Copilot channel and the pricing date.
  • VS Code frames show the extension (AI Credits Analyzer v0.8.0); Claude Desktop frames show the standalone app (Agent Cost Analyzer v0.12.0). Both tools are in both products.
  • The "View as table" control and "Show 5 assumptions" are collapsed here; the replay's assumptions A1–A5 open as a list.
  • The Unknown-price state (a model with no price) is on the Increment 3 states board.
