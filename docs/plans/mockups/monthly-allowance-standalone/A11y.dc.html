Gauge button: name, role and label

A button with aria-haspopup="dialog". The meter and the level color are aria-hidden; every level is in the words. The level sentence comes first when near or over. The label is the same at every width, and the visible text keeps a short level word (near, over) at narrow widths.

Details dialog: Enter or Space on the gauge opens it; focus lands on Close; Tab cycles inside; Escape or Close returns focus to the gauge.

1Close2Recount3Change allowance

Settings dialog: focus lands on the allowance field. Cancel, Escape and a successful Save return focus to the gauge. After Save the gauge text updates from the PUT response and a polite live region says “Monthly allowance saved.” After Remove: “Monthly allowance removed.”

1Allowance2Reset day3Warn at4Remove allowance5Cancel6Save

A server 400 sets aria-invalid on the named field, shows its message beside it (with a ✕ mark, not color alone), links it with aria-describedby and moves focus to the first invalid field.

Contrast and color

  • Text uses the ledger text and muted roles (4.5:1). The meter marks use the existing accent, warning (status-interrupted) and error (status-error) marks at 3:1 or more; no new token.
  • Near limit and over differ by words first, then by the threshold tick and the overflow cap on the meter.
  • At least is drawn with a hatched, dashed open end; the figure is never drawn exact.
  • Not set is muted text on its own border (4.5:1), not a disabled control.
